WAREKNIT LOGISTICS .COM
Tech-Enabled 3PL Website — UI/UX Design Brief
Project: Wareknit Logistics WebsiteLocation: Dallas, Texas, USABusiness: Technology-Enabled Third-Party Logistics (3PL)Design Stage: UI/UX DesignPrimary Goal: Create a premium, technology-forward 3PL website that generates qualified leads and establishes Wareknit Logistics as a modern logistics + WMS + AI company.
01. PROJECT BACKGROUND
Wareknit Logistics is a new 3PL operation being established in Dallas, Texas.
The client is acquiring a warehouse facility and building the Wareknit Logistics operation around a strong background in Warehouse Management Systems (WMS), logistics technology and supply-chain solutions.
The long-term vision is not simply to operate a warehouse.
The business intends to combine:
PHYSICAL LOGISTICS
WMS TECHNOLOGY
AI-POWERED SOLUTIONS
DATA & VISIBILITY
3PL SERVICES
The website therefore needs to communicate that Wareknit is a technology-enabled logistics partner, rather than a traditional warehouse operator.
02. THE BIG IDEA
Don't sell "warehouse space."
Sell:
A smarter logistics operation.
Wareknit should communicate:
We don't just store and ship your products. We connect your inventory, warehouse operations and technology into one smarter fulfillment ecosystem.
The website should make the customer understand:
Physical warehouse → WMS → Data → AI → Better decisions → Better fulfillment
