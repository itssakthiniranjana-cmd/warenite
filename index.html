// OPERATIONAL LIFECYCLE

One Connected Flow.

From inbound receiving to real-time analytics and predictive optimization—we elevate fulfillment from a cost center into a growth engine.

STEP 01

Receive

Inbound freight arrives, pallets are barcoded, and automated dimensioners log specs into WMS within 2 hours.

STEP 02

Store

Algorithmically slotted into high-bay racking or dedicated pick bins based on velocity and physical characteristics.

STEP 03

Manage

Perpetual cycle counts, lot expiration monitoring, and real-time inventory synchronization across your sales channels.

STEP 04

Fulfill

Wave picking with RF scanners, automated packing verification, and custom branded inserts with 99.8% precision.

STEP 05

Ship

Multi-carrier rate shopping across FedEx, UPS, USPS & freight linehauls for optimal transit speed and cost efficiency.

STEP 06 · TECH

Analyze & Optimize

Post-fulfillment telemetry feeds into AI models to refine future inventory placement and carrier allocations.

Receive → Store → Manage → Fulfill → Ship → Analyze & Optimize
